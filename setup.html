Setup & Editing Guide

The album pages now have separate buttons for Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Amazon Music and YouTube for every individual song, plus the same choices at album level.

What to replace: Anything beginning with YOUR_ is a placeholder. Replace it with the real URL for that album or song.

For each album

Open album-1.html through album-4.html. Change the band name, album title, description, album-level links and track names/links.

Each song

Every track has five destinations: Spotify, Apple Music, iTunes, Amazon Music and YouTube. Customers can choose where they want to listen, watch or buy.

Important

Apple Music and iTunes can use different URLs, so each has its own field. Amazon Music is also used for the purchase link when you want customers to buy the song.
